Overview

Supported the integration of a standardized Clinical Foundations curriculum into an LMS environment by working cross-functionally with training and development teams and an external instructional design vendor.

The Challenge

The organization needed a more structured and consistent onboarding experience for clinical staff, with improved alignment across training content and delivery methods. Integrating an external vendor's curriculum into an existing LMS while maintaining quality, consistency, and organizational readiness required careful coordination and structured QA processes.

My Role

Technical Project Manager supporting curriculum integration, change management planning, vendor coordination, and quality assurance testing.

Strategy & Execution

  • Collaborated with training and development teams to align curriculum with organizational learning goals

  • Supported integration of curriculum into LMS environment

  • Coordinated with external instructional design vendor throughout the project lifecycle

  • Spearheaded change management planning to support adoption and rollout readiness

  • Led alpha and beta testing cycles with 15–20 licensed clinical experts

Impact

  • Improved consistency across clinical onboarding materials

  • Strengthened alignment between training teams and operational delivery

  • Established structured QA feedback cycles with licensed clinical experts

  • Supported readiness for organization-wide rollout

Reflection

This work strengthened my ability to operate at the intersection of systems coordination, quality assurance, and structured implementation. It also reinforced my interest in how digital learning systems are built and maintained — and sparked my transition into software development.
