My Story

About Me

From managing systems to building and validating them.

My Journey

I began my career in project management, where I developed a strong foundation in structured execution, cross-functional coordination, and systems thinking.

Over time, I led initiatives involving training integration, curriculum rollout, vendor collaboration, and quality assurance testing. Much of my work required identifying gaps, validating processes, and ensuring successful implementation across teams and systems.

As I grew in my role, I became increasingly interested in how digital systems are built, tested, and improved.

Today, I operate in a hybrid space — bringing the discipline of a project manager and the curiosity of a developer to every challenge I take on.

Technical Project Manager

Structured execution and cross-functional leadership

Systems Coordinator

QA-Minded Thinker

Validation, testing, and gap identification

Aspiring Developer

Building front-end skills with HTML, CSS, JS & React

Why Development

I'm especially interested in QA and front-end development

Because they combine structure, logic, and user experience in a way that aligns with how I naturally approach problem solving. Testing isn't just checking boxes — it's understanding how a system should work and making sure it does.

Structured Thinking

Every project I've led has required me to think in systems — inputs, outputs, dependencies, risks. That's exactly how good code works too.

User Focus

Whether managing a program or building an interface, my work has always been about the people on the receiving end of the experience.

Continuous Growth

I'm not starting from scratch — I'm adding a new skill set to a strong professional foundation. That combination is rare and valuable.
