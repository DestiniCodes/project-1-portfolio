Case Study

Oregon Financial Literacy Initiative

Led coordination of a $150,000 state-funded pilot serving 112 youth, focused on financial education, structured incentives, and impact tracking across multiple systems.

Overview

The Oregon Financial Literacy Initiative was a state-funded pilot program designed to provide structured financial education and savings incentives to youth participants. The program operated across multiple cohorts and required coordination across funding systems, participant tracking tools, and reporting structures.

The Challenge

Managing a state-funded program at this scale required careful coordination of stakeholder expectations, compliance requirements, stipend workflows, and data tracking — all while maintaining consistent delivery quality across multiple cohorts and participant groups.

My Role

Lead Project Manager responsible for program coordination, cross-functional collaboration, stakeholder alignment, stipend workflow oversight, savings match tracking, and reporting.

Strategy & Execution

  • Coordinated program planning and delivery across multiple cohorts and stakeholder groups

  • Managed stipend workflows and savings match tracking across participant records

  • Aligned internal teams and external stakeholders around program timelines and compliance requirements

  • Maintained accurate impact tracking and reporting across systems

Impact

  • Successfully coordinated delivery of financial literacy programming to 112 youth across 3 cohorts

  • Maintained compliance and reporting accuracy for a $150,000 state-funded initiative

  • Established structured workflows for stipend distribution and savings match tracking

  • Strengthened stakeholder alignment and cross-functional communication throughout program delivery

Reflection

This project reinforced my ability to manage complex, multi-stakeholder initiatives with competing priorities and tight compliance requirements. It also deepened my understanding of how systems — whether financial, data, or operational — need to be intentionally designed and maintained to produce consistent, measurable outcomes.
